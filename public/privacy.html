Privacy Policy

Mail Studio lets you write, personalize, and send email from your Gmail account.

Google data and permissions

Firebase Authentication provides your verified Google email address and account identifiers. Mail Studio uses these to sign you in, keep your workspace private, and match the Gmail account you connect to the account you used to sign in.

When you connect Gmail, Mail Studio requests permission to send email. It uses that permission to deliver the messages you put in your sending queue. It does not access your Gmail inbox or contacts.

What the workspace stores

Mail Studio stores your draft, recipients and imported personalization fields, any PDF you attach, and delivery progress on the server so you can return to the campaign. Gmail access and refresh tokens are held in server memory and cleared when the server restarts. Mail Studio sends your message and attachment to Gmail for delivery to the recipients you choose.

Mail Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting Gmail and requesting deletion

Pause the queue before disconnecting Gmail. You can also revoke Mail Studio's Gmail access in your Google Account connections. Signing out ends your browser session. An active queue keeps running until it finishes or you pause it.

To request deletion of your saved workspace or ask a privacy question, contact the support email shown on Mail Studio's Google consent screen. Revoking Gmail access does not delete your saved workspace.
